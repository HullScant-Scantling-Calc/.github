# HullScant - Scantling checks for plating on a hull you already drew

## HullScant Highlights

- **Rule, Not A Render:** HullScant scantling calc applies a structural rule you select to panels and stiffeners of a boat you already designed.
- **How Thick The Plate:** HullScant plate thickness is the output builders wait for, once span, pressure, and material are the ones on your drawing.
- **What Section The Stiffener Needs:** HullScant stiffener size checks modulus and spacing on members you intend to fit.
- **ISO And Similar Yacht Paths:** HullScant structural check is at home with small-craft rules (for example ISO-style yacht scantlings) rather than a full class finite-element ship.
- **Numbers After The Hull File:** This is not a surface modeler. Bring dimensions from DELFTship, FREEship, or a lines plan you own.

## What Is HullScant

HullScant is a Windows calculation desk for yacht and small-craft structure. You already have a hull: stations, spans, materials. HullScant scantling calc asks whether the plate and the stiffener meet the rule you are building to. HullScant plate thickness and HullScant stiffener size are the answers. HullScant structural check is the session - panel by panel, not a pretty 3D walkthrough.

Designers open it when a garage build or a small yard needs a documented thickness, not a guess from the last boat. Class FEA on a tanker is a different world. Here the craft is a sailing yacht, a power cat, a work skiff you have the right to engineer. Inputs must be honest: a wrong span makes a wrong plate.

Use it on vessels you design, own, or were hired to scantling. Keep the calc sheets with the drawings. Do not rubber-stamp a hull you have not measured.

## Starting with HullScant

Prerequisites: A PC you administer, a hull drawing you own or were hired to check, and the rule you will build to.

1. **Prepare:** Take spans, materials, and design category from the drawings, not from memory.
2. **Configure:** Select that rule in HullScant scantling calc and set units to match the lofting set.
3. **Test:** Run one bottom panel and compare HullScant plate thickness with a hand estimate you already trust.
4. **Refine:** Add stiffeners and confirm HullScant stiffener size against the spacing on the plan.
5. **Apply:** Work through remaining panels of the hull you drew.
6. **Maintain:** Re-run HullScant structural check if the designer changes span or category, and archive the PDF with that revision.

- **ISO-Style Yacht You Are Building:** HullScant scantling calc on a hull you have the right to engineer.
- **Power Skiff Bottom Panels:** HullScant plate thickness where slamming pressure is the point.
- **Catamaran Bridgedeck You Drew:** Check the span you actually modeled.
- **Amateur Build With A Hired Checker:** The engineer runs HullScant structural check; the builder does not guess.
- **Not A Substitute For Class FEA:** Ships that need full class stay in those tools.
- **After A Sheer Or Beam Change:** Re-calc, do not keep the old plate note.
- **Keep Rule Edition With The File:** HullScant stiffener size is only as current as the rule you selected.
